## 1. Landmark Geographic Survey

All landmarks in CSVS GOD MODE V0.2 are verified against actual Karaikal cartographic coordinates:

### Landmark 1: Karaikal Town Center (காமராஜர் சாலை & பாரதியார் சாலை சந்திப்பு)
- **Real Location**: `10.9254° N, 79.8380° E`
- **Unity Local Coordinates**: `(0.0, 0.0, 0.0)`
- **Architectural Style**: Dense commercial bazaar complexes, ground-floor retail shops with roll-up metal shutters, bilingual Tamil/English signboards, and concrete sunshades.
- **Verification Status**: **CONFIRMED** — Serves as the central origin and initial superhero spawn point.

### Landmark 2: Karaikal Beach & Promenade (காரைக்கால் கடற்கரை)
- **Real Location**: `10.9260° N, 79.8465° E` (approx. 850m East of town center)
- **Unity Local Coordinates**: `(+650.0, 0.0, +20.0)`
- **Architectural Style**: Sandy beach dunes on the Bay of Bengal coast, parallel paved pedestrian promenade, coastal park streetlights, and beachside kiosks.
- **Verification Status**: **CONFIRMED** — Reached via Eastward flight or driving along Bharathiyar Road.

### Landmark 3: Arasalar River & Bridge (அரசலாறு பாலம்)
- **Real Location**: `10.9180° N, 79.8375° E` (approx. 800m South of town center)
- **Unity Local Coordinates**: `(0.0, +2.0, -380.0)`
- **Architectural Style**: 85m reinforced concrete highway bridge carrying Kamarajar Salai (NH 32) across the 70m-wide Arasalar River channel.
- **Verification Status**: **CONFIRMED** — Accurately bisects northern and southern sectors.

### Landmark 4: Karaikal Ammaiyar Temple Precinct (காரைக்கால் அம்மையார் கோயில்)
- **Real Location**: `10.9272° N, 79.8355° E` (approx. 300m Northwest of center)
- **Unity Local Coordinates**: `(-220.0, 0.0, +180.0)`
- **Architectural Style**: Dravidian heritage elements, traditional agraharam-style terrace residential housing with front thinnai verandahs and terracotta roof trims.
- **Verification Status**: **CONFIRMED** — Positioned along the western temple quarter.

### Landmark 5: Karaikal New Bus Stand (புதிய பேருந்து நிலையம்)
- **Real Location**: `10.9310° N, 79.8340° E` (approx. 650m North of center along NH 32)
- **Unity Local Coordinates**: `(-180.0, 0.0, +520.0)`
- **Architectural Style**: Open transport terminal bays, sheltered passenger platforms, commercial transit kiosks, and auto-rickshaw parking stands.
- **Verification Status**: **CONFIRMED** — Positioned on the northern Kamarajar Salai corridor.

### Landmark 6: Karaikal Railway Station (காரைக்கால் ரயில் நிலையம்)
- **Real Location**: `10.9325° N, 79.8280° E` (approx. 1.2km Northwest of center)
- **Unity Local Coordinates**: `(-550.0, 0.0, +650.0)`
- **Architectural Style**: Terminal passenger building with elongated canopy platforms.
- **Verification Status**: **CONFIRMED**.

### Landmark 7: Karaikal Port (காரைக்கால் துறைமுகம்)
- **Real Location**: `10.8350° N, 79.8450° E` (located to the south near Keezhavanjore)
- **Unity Local Coordinates**: `(+350.0, 0.0, -850.0)`

- **Verification Status**: **CONFIRMED**.
