# BUILDING MODEL INVENTORY — KARAIKAL V0.2.2

## 1. Overview

The V0.2.2 architecture system utilizes procedural multi-element mesh generation to create authentic South Indian coastal town structures. Every building is assembled from modular architectural components rather than monolithic colored cubes.

---

## 2. Architectural Typologies

### Typology A: Traditional Franco-Tamil / Chettinad Residential House
- **Inspiration**: French colonial quarters and traditional Tamil residential streets (Bharathiar Road, Rue Suffren).
- **Key Features**:
  - Ground-floor raised open veranda (*Thinnai*) with carved support pillars.
  - Clay Mangalore terracotta pitched tiled roof ($30^\circ$ pitch) over lime-plastered masonry.
  - Pastel wash exterior walls (mint green, pale saffron, French ochre, soft turquoise).
  - Ornate carved teakwood entrance doorways with brass knockers.
  - Slatted wooden window shutters (*Jallies*) with projecting drip mouldings (*Chajjas*).

### Typology B: Commercial Shophouse / Bazaar Complex
- **Inspiration**: Karaikal Market Street and Church Street bazaars.
- **Key Features**:
  - Ground floor: Galvanized metal rolling shutters, open storefront displays, and canvas awnings (striped red/white or blue/yellow).
  - Upper floors: Commercial offices, storage, and residential flats with recessed verandas and parapet railings.
  - Exterior details: Tamil & English signage boards (*Bata*, *Aavin Milk*, *Bakery*, *Textiles*), wall-mounted air conditioner outdoor compressor units, and electrical meter conduits.

### Typology C: Sacred Dravidian Gopuram & Temple Architecture
- **Inspiration**: Karaikal Ammaiyar Temple and regional coastal Kovils.
- **Key Features**:
  - Stepped pyramidal *Gopuram* entrance tower (5 tiers) with sculpted ornamental tier recesses and finial *Kalasams*.
  - Enclosed courtyard with stone pillared *Mandapam* hall.
  - Adjacent sacred rectangular temple tank (*Theertham*) with stone perimeter steps (*Ghats*).
  - White and red vertical sacred stucco wall stripes (*Pattai*).

### Typology D: Civic, Port & Industrial Warehouses
- **Inspiration**: Karaikal Port perimeter, Railway Yard, and Municipal offices.
- **Key Features**:
  - Corrugated metal dual-pitch warehouse sheds with structural steel gables.
  - High-clearance cargo bay roller doors.
  - Reinforced concrete administrative block with flat roof and communication antennas.

---

## 3. Rooftop Utility & Detail Inventory

All procedural residential and commercial buildings feature randomized rooftop utility props to establish visual believability:
1. **Sintex Overhead Water Tanks**: Cylindrical water reservoirs in signature industrial black and white polyethylene with embossed ribbing.
2. **Television Antennas & Dish Receivers**: Parabolic DTH antennas and wireframe Yagi television aerials.
3. **Stairwell Penthouse Structures (*Mumty*)**: Brick masonry rooftop enclosures with weather-sealed iron doors.
4. **Concrete Parapet Walls**: Boundary railings with drainage scuppers and drying line posts.

---

## 4. Materials & Shaders

- **Shader**: Universal Render Pipeline `URP/Lit` and `URP/Simple Lit`.
- **Textures**: Procedurally synthesized diffuse, bump/normal, and ambient occlusion textures.
- **Color Palettes**: Regionally accurate South Indian palette (Tamil terracotta, lime wash white, French colonial ochre, turquoise, deep teak).
