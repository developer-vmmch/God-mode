# ARCHITECTURE & VEHICLE MODEL INVENTORY — CSVS GOD MODE V0.3

## 1. Architectural Models (Blender Produced)

### Commercial Bazaar Shophouse (`Commercial_Bazaar.fbx`)
- **Dimensions**: $10.0\text{ m}$ (width) $\times 12.0\text{ m}$ (depth) $\times 12.5\text{ m}$ (overall height to parapet).
- **Storefronts**: 3 commercial ground-floor bays equipped with corrugated metal rolling shutters and protective concrete drip mouldings (*chajjas*).
- **Fascia Signboard**: $9.5\text{ m} \times 0.85\text{ m}$ prominent retail signage space across the front facade for bilingual Tamil/English merchant typography.
- **Fenestration**: 6 upper-floor residential/office windows featuring protruding concrete sunshades designed to reflect coastal monsoon protection architecture.
- **Rooftop Infrastructure**:
  - Perimeter parapet wall in French colonial blue trim (`#2673A6`).
  - 2 cylindrical high-density black polyethylene *Sintex* water storage tanks mounted on dedicated reinforced concrete plinths.

## 2. Vehicle Models (Blender Produced)

### South Indian Auto-Rickshaw (`AutoRickshaw.fbx`)
- **Chassis Configuration**: 3-wheel delta steering arrangement (1 front steered wheel, 2 rear drive wheels).
- **Cab Geometry**:
  - Yellow canvas weather canopy (`#F2BF0D`).
  - Black pressed-steel passenger tub (`#141417`).
  - Front steering apron with raked windshield glass.
  - Single front chrome tubular steering fork with positive caster angle.
- **Lighting & Trim**:
  - Central circular chrome headlight with protective outer cage.
  - Left and right rear rectangular brake/tail lamp assemblies.
  - Chromed wheel rims with all-terrain tire treads.

### Commuter Motorcycle (`Motorcycle.fbx`)
- **Chassis Configuration**: Dual cradle steel frame with 150cc engine casing.
- **Bodywork**:
  - Gloss red sculpted teardrop fuel tank.
  - Textured black stepped dual passenger seat.
  - Front telescopic suspension forks with circular chrome headlight and raised commuter handlebars.
  - Chromed exhaust pipe with thermal guard heat shield.
  - 18-inch front and rear spoked chrome rims with rubber road tires.
