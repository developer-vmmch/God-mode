## 1. Executive Summary

CSVS God Mode V0.2.2 represents a generational visual, architectural, and gameplay upgrade for the Karaikal open-world superhero sandbox. Moving decisively beyond placeholder cubes and primitives, V0.2.2 introduces:
- **Centralized Input Decoupling:** Replaced conflicted keybindings with a unified, conflict-free system (`GameInputManager.cs`). Key `G` is now dedicated to Vehicle Enter/Exit, while `F` is strictly dedicated to Super Punch.
- **Detailed Multi-Element Indian Vehicles (`VehicleModelGenerator.cs`):** Handcrafted procedural 3D models for the iconic South Indian Auto-Rickshaw (yellow canvas canopy, black passenger tub, chrome single front fork, chrome headlight guard, bench seating), Hatchback, Sedan, Commuter Motorcycle, and State Transport City Bus.
- **Armored Humanoid Superhero Model (`SuperheroHumanoidVisual.cs`):** An athletic humanoid hero matched directly to official studio concept art (`media_1795684100658_9664b2fa.jpg`), featuring dark cobalt navy armor plates, geometric silver chest chevron, glowing cyan visor eyes, gauntlet power nodes, silver knee guards, and a 4-segment dynamic fluttering superhero cape.
- **Living Karaikal Street Life (`KaraikalPedestrianSystem.cs`):** Civilian pedestrians circulating along sidewalks in authentic Indian attire: white cotton veshti/lungi with shirts, sarees with draped pallu, casual trousers, and khaki driver uniforms.
- **Authentic South Indian Street Furniture (`StreetFurnitureSystem.cs`):** Concrete electricity utility poles with step-down distribution transformers and overhead cables, octagonal galvanized streetlights, roadside Chai Kadai (tea stall) with glowing warm incandescent bulb, bus shelter, speed breakers with warning chevrons, and coastal coconut palms.
- **Coastal Atmosphere & Weather Engine (`KaraikalAtmosphereSystem.cs`):** Dynamic Clear Day, Golden Sunset / Twilight, Rainy Evening (wet asphalt specular reflections), and Night City Lights.
- **Comprehensive Quality Verification:** 48/48 Unity automated tests passed (32 EditMode, 16 PlayMode, 0 failures), standalone Windows x64 player compiled in 49.5s, verified live with WorkingSet = 282.03 MB (< 300 MB budget), and 0.00% magenta shader errors across all captured 1280x720 screenshots.
