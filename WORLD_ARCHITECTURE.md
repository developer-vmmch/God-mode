## 2. District Profiles

### District 1: Karaikal Town Center
- **Center**: Intersection of Kamarajar Salai (NH 32) and Bharathiyar Road.
- **Atmosphere**: Bustling commercial heart, dense 3–4 story pastel bazaar complexes, Tamil/English commercial signage, roadside tea stalls, auto-rickshaw parking bays.
- **Role**: Initial superhero spawn point, primary ground navigation hub.

### District 2: Karaikal Beach & Promenade
- **Center**: Coastal belt along Beach Road ($X = +550\,\text{m}$ to $+900\,\text{m}$).
- **Atmosphere**: Sandy dunes overlooking the Bay of Bengal, paved pedestrian promenade, coastal park streetlights, sea breeze.
- **Role**: High-speed coastal flight, open combat space, vehicle cruising along Beach Road.

### District 3: Arasalar Riverfront & Bridge
- **Center**: $Z = -380\,\text{m}$, crossing Kamarajar Salai.
- **Atmosphere**: 70m-wide tidal river channel flowing eastward into the sea, spanned by the 85m reinforced concrete Arasalar Bridge with concrete piers and crash railings.
- **Role**: Major highway landmark, aerial flight corridor along the river, bridge defense / destruction arena.

### District 4: Heritage & Temple Precinct
- **Center**: Northwest of town center ($X = -220\,\text{m}, Z = +180\,\text{m}$).
- **Atmosphere**: Historical cultural quarter surrounding the Karaikal Ammaiyar Temple precinct, traditional South Indian terrace houses with compound walls and front verandahs (thinnai).
- **Role**: Cultural identity, ground-level navigation, protected landmark zone.

### District 5: Transport Hub
- **Center**: North Kamarajar Salai ($Z = +480\,\text{m}$ to $+650\,\text{m}$).
- **Atmosphere**: Inter-district New Bus Stand and Southern Railway approach, large transit platforms, parking aprons.
- **Role**: Ground transit hub, traffic density node.

### District 6: Port & Industrial Zone
- **Center**: South of Arasalar River ($Z = -600\,\text{m}$ to $-850\,\text{m}, X = +160\,\text{m}$ to $+400\,\text{m}$).
- **Atmosphere**: Corrugated steel warehouses, cargo loading bays, multi-colored stacked shipping containers.
- **Role**: Heavy object interaction, vehicle collisions, container grabbing/throwing combat.

### District 7: Rural Outskirts
- **Center**: Western arterial ($X < -350\,\text{m}$) along Thirunallar Highway.
- **Atmosphere**: Lush delta paddy field terrain, open horizons, village brick houses.
- **Role**: High-speed flight acceleration, long-distance driving.

### District 8: Fictional Modern Coastal Expansion
- **Center**: Northeast coastal sector ($X = +250\,\text{m}$ to $+450\,\text{m}, Z = +600\,\text{m}$ to $+850\,\text{m}$).
- **Atmosphere**: Modern corporate glass towers (8–13 floors), helipads, reflecting the visual ambition of GTA V / GTA VI.
- **Role**: Vertical superhero flight challenges, skyscraper destruction arenas.
